Installing Echo One
-------------------

1. Drag EchoOne.app to your Applications folder.

2. First launch: do NOT double-click — right-click the app and choose Open.
   macOS will warn about an unverified developer. Click Open to proceed.
   After this first time you can open it normally.

3. Grant Accessibility and Microphone permissions when prompted — both are
   required for dictation to work.
